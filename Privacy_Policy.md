# Privacy Policy

**Pocket Ledger**
Last updated: February 2026

---

## The Short Version

Pocket Ledger does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

---

## What Data the App Stores

Pocket Ledger stores the following data locally on your device:

- Account names, types, and balances
- Transaction records (amounts, dates, categories, tags, notes)
- Categories, tags, and templates you create
- App preferences and settings

All data is written to your device's local storage using iOS file protection (`NSFileProtectionComplete`). This means your data is encrypted at rest and inaccessible while your device is locked.

---

## What Data Leaves Your Device

**None — by default.**

Pocket Ledger has no internet connection, no backend server, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not require an account to use.

The only time any data leaves your device is when **you explicitly choose to export it**:

- **JSON Backup** — You can export a full backup file to any location you choose (your Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, etc.). This is entirely under your control.
- **CSV Export** — You can export a date-range report of your transactions to a spreadsheet file. Again, the destination is your choice entirely.

Neither export is automatic, scheduled, or sent anywhere without your direct action.

---

## Notifications

If you create a Template with a scheduled reminder, Pocket Ledger will use iOS local notifications to alert you at the time you set. These notifications are generated entirely on-device. No notification data is sent to any server.

You can disable notifications for Pocket Ledger at any time in your iOS Settings.

---

## Third-Party Services

Pocket Ledger uses no third-party services, SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics platforms. There are no trackers of any kind.

---

## Children's Privacy

Pocket Ledger does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. As no data leaves the device, the app presents no particular risk in this regard.
